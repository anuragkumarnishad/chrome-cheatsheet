Chrome use karte ho roz, par kya aapko ye 5 shortcuts pata hain? 👇

Maine Chrome ki 100+ shortcut keys aur poori Settings ka ek Hinglish cheat-sheet banaya hai — 10 slides mein sab kuch:

Slide 2 → Tab & Window: Ctrl+Shift+T se band hua tab wapas (sabse life-saving key!)
Slide 3 → Address Bar magic: site + Tab = usi site ka search; Ctrl+K = kahin se bhi search
Slide 4 → Page ke andar: Ctrl+Shift+R = hard refresh, F7 = caret browsing
Slide 5 → Mouse tricks: Alt+Click = link download, Ctrl+Click = background tab
Slide 6 → Tools: Shift+Esc = Chrome ka apna Task Manager (RAM khaane wala tab pakdo)
Slide 7 → Settings tour: chrome://performance (Memory Saver), chrome://privacy, sab URLs ke saath
Slide 8 → Privacy & speed must-dos: 3rd-party cookies block, password checkup
Slide 9 → Secret pages: chrome://flags, chrome://net-internals…
Slide 10 → Pro tips: pin tabs, tab groups, vertical tabs, install-as-app

Bonus: kuch common galat shortcuts jo log maante hain —
❌ Ctrl+, se Settings (Windows) → ✅ Alt+F phir S (ya chrome://settings)
❌ Ctrl+Shift+Esc → ✅ Shift+Esc (Chrome Task Manager)
❌ F3 = next match → ✅ Ctrl+G

Saare shortcuts Google ki official help page se verify kiye hain.
